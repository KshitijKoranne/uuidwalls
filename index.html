Questions

Honest answers

Does this send my UUID anywhere?
No. Everything runs in your browser. Your UUID is never transmitted. There is no backend, no analytics on your input.
What resolution will my wallpaper be?
You choose. Screen res matches your display. 2K exports at 2560×1440. 4K at 3840×2160. Mobile at 1290×2796. All crisp, all local.
Can two machines get the same wall?
Only if they share a UUID — which is cryptographically impossible by design. Your wall is provably yours.
Will my wall look the same next year?
Yes. The algorithm is deterministic and versioned. Same UUID, same result, always. It is not random.
Does it only work on Mac?
No. Any device with a UUID works — Mac, Windows, Linux, iPhone, Android. Even a serial number or any string produces a wall.
Is a native app coming?
Yes. Native apps will read your UUID automatically and set the wallpaper with one tap. Web first, apps soon.
